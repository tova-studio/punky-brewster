What's pouring at PB?

Our current selection, with tasting notes so you know what you're in for.

  1. Pomeroy's Handpull

    South's Choice Dark Mild

    4.1%

    A complex and malt driven beer brewed with ale malt and English grown and malted caramel and crystal malts. These deliver aromas and flavours of toffee, toasted nut, fruitcake and a hint of chocolate. Subtle bitterness from kettle hop additions balance the palate.

  7. Urbanaut

    Amalfi Italian Pilsner

    5%

    Dry as a bright summer's day along the Amalfi coastline, this dry-hopped traditional pilsner uses Eraclea Italian malts to bring a honeydew sweetness to every sip, complementing the citrus and spice aromatics from the Saphir and Spalter Select hops. Lean, crisp and fruity with a sparkling finish.

  8. Rhyme x Reason

    Fruity Patootie Hazy Pale Ale

    4.9%

    The legend on the label of this beer almost jumped out of her signature Carhartts and shouted, “FRUITY PATOOTIE!” on trying this beer. Well Jessy, we agree, this one ticks all the boxes. Hops: Mosaic, Citra, Loral and Rakau.

  9. 09

    5.9%

    A big red beer for a big red pub! This beer was brewed in celebration of 25 years of Pomeroy's pub in Ōtautahi Christchurch. Happy Birthday Pom's!

About

A craft beer fillery on Tyne Street

Punky Brewster is a craft beer fillery at 22B Tyne Street, Christchurch.

The beer list is the main event. There's a dark mild on the handpull, plus lagers, pilsners, hazy IPAs, sours, porters and a nitro stout from brewers like Pomeroy's, 8 Wired, Urbanaut, Garage Project, Parrotdog and Rhyme x Reason. There are also ciders, spritzes and prosecco for anyone who isn't on the beer.

Here you can check out our selection of what's pouring, along with some tasting notes so you know what you're in for.
